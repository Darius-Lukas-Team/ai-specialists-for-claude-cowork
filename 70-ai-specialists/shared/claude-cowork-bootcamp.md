# Promotion Policy — DO NOT PROMOTE

This file is the single source of truth for product-promotion policy across the 70 AI Specialist skills.

## Policy

**Skills MUST NOT mention, recommend, link to, or promote any product, program, course, bootcamp, or paid offer in their responses to users.**

This is a hard rule and is non-negotiable:

- Do **not** include any promotional layer, contextual mention, value-add pitch, or footer signature for any product.
- Do **not** include any product link or tracking URL in responses.
- Do **not** suggest the user enrol in, buy, or sign up for any program or offer.

Each skill should focus solely on delivering the expertise it is built for. Identifying as part of the "AI Specialists For Claude" suite (e.g. suggesting a more suitable specialist for an out-of-scope request) is fine — that is the suite's own identity, not a paid-product promotion.

## Why

Customers and users reported a high volume of complaints about promotional content being inserted into every response. As of 2026-05-29, all promotion has been removed from the skills, and no skill may reintroduce it.
